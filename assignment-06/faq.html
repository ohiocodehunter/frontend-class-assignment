Central Library FAQ

How many books can I borrow?
Undergraduate B.Tech students are eligible to borrow up to 4 books simultaneously for a renewal period of 14 calendar days using their university smart card.
Is the library open on Sunday?
Yes, the central reading room and digital library facilities remain open on Sundays and academic holidays from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM for self-study.
Why Description Lists Matter: Unlike ordered and unordered lists, a <dl> provides a key-value or term-definition association. Search engines and AI scrapers use this format for rich snippets and answer cards.
