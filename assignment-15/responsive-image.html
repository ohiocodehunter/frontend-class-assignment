Assignment 15 • Task 2: Fluid Responsive Image

Aim: Ensure image assets scale proportionally without clipping or overflowing their container using max-width: 100% and height: auto.

File: responsive-image.html
CSS Rules: max-width: 100%; height: auto;
Asset: images/sample.jpg

Fluid Media Display

Rendered Output

University Campus Workspace

Image adapts dynamically. When the screen narrows below its native pixel width, it scales down proportionally rather than bursting out of the layout bounds.

Why max-width: 100% Beats width: 100%: Using width: 100% forces small images to stretch beyond their natural resolution, leading to pixelation. max-width: 100% allows an image to stay at its crisp native size on large screens while shrinking smoothly on small screens.
